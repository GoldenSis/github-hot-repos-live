📄 FRESH PAPERS
ARXIV CS.IR · OCT 2024 (TRENDING OCT 2026)
LightRAG: Simple and Fast Retrieval-Augmented Generation

The paper behind this week's #07 repo. Introduces a graph-based retrieval system that stores entities and relationships explicitly, enabling dual-level (specific + abstract) retrieval that flat vector search misses. Benchmarked on legal, agricultural, and CS corpora; outperforms naive RAG and GraphRAG on multi-hop queries while being faster to index. Over 500 HF upvotes; the GitHub repo crossed 14k stars this week. arXiv:2410.05779 →

ARXIV CS.AI · OCT 2026
TradingAgents: Multi-Agents LLM Financial Trading Framework

The companion paper for this week's #02 / #10 repo. Proposes a trading-firm simulation where LLM agents with specialist roles (fundamentals, sentiment, technicals, risk) debate before executing. Compared to single-agent baselines on S&P 500 historical data; the multi-agent debate protocol improves risk-adjusted returns (Sharpe ratio +0.4) and maximum drawdown. Open-sourced under Apache 2.0; code matches the paper's tables. Repo + paper →

ARXIV CS.CL · OCT 2026
STORM: Synthesis of Topic Outlines through Retrieval and Multi-perspective Question Asking

Stanford's paper demonstrating that long-form Wikipedia-quality article generation requires simulating diverse expert perspectives before writing, not just retrieving facts. The "pre-writing" stage — where virtual experts formulate questions from their viewpoints — reduces hallucination by 34% versus direct generation. Co-STORM extends this to interactive human collaboration. Used as the basis for the #08 repo which now has 15k+ stars. arXiv:2402.14207 →
