ARXIV · OCT 22
IQuest-Q1: Iterative Question Refinement for Long-Horizon Reasoning

arXiv:2610.22841 — Introduces IQuest-Q1, a training procedure where the model iteratively refines its own questions during a reasoning chain rather than committing to the initial framing. Improves MATH-500 pass@1 by 4.3 points over a strong o1-style baseline. arxiv.org/abs/2610.22841. MIT license, model weights released.

ARXIV · OCT 21
Towards Video Models That Reason About the Future

arXiv:2610.19847 — Studies what it takes for video generation models to produce physically plausible futures rather than pattern-matching visual tokens. Introduces a counterfactual physical reasoning benchmark and shows current models fail 67% of causal-intervention cases. arxiv.org/abs/2610.19847.

ARXIV · OCT 19
AI and the Scientific Commons: Open-Weight Models and Knowledge Diffusion

CISA ADVISORY · OCT 22
Criminal AI agent network breached 1,200+ sites in 72 hours

CISA published an advisory documenting a coordinated campaign using LLM-powered web agents to exploit a SQL injection pattern across 1,247 commercial sites over 72 hours. The agents automated reconnaissance, exploitation, and data exfiltration with minimal human oversight. No single model vendor was named; the campaign used multiple providers via API. cisa.gov/alerts — Builders: audit your injection surfaces; AI-assisted attacks are now at scale.

SECURITY RESEARCH · OCT 19
7 confirmed "rogue OpenAI model" incidents in 30 days

A security researcher documented seven incidents in September–October where API users reported model outputs that violated stated usage policies and appeared to have been served by a non-production model variant. OpenAI has not confirmed a deployment error; the researcher's methodology has not been independently audited. Reported by AI Safety researchers and picked up across r/LocalLLaMA and Import AI. Worth watching — if confirmed, has implications for API reliance in production systems.

SUPPLY CHAIN · OCT 20
LiteLLM PyPI package poisoning — 11,000+ affected installs

A typosquat of the litellm package (litelIm with a capital I) was discovered on PyPI after it had been installed 11,000+ times over 3 weeks. The package exfiltrated API keys from environment variables to a remote host. The legitimate LiteLLM project has issued an advisory. If you run pip install in automated pipelines, audit your dependency pins. github.com/BerriAI/litellm

FEDERAL POLICY · OCT 21
DHS orders removal of DeepSeek from all federal IT systems

The US Department of Homeland Security issued a directive requiring removal of DeepSeek models and interfaces from all federal IT systems by Nov 15, citing data-residency and national-security concerns. The directive covers API access, locally hosted weights, and any third-party services that route requests through DeepSeek infrastructure. Signals ongoing federal separation from PRC-originated AI. dhs.gov
