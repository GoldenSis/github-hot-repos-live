## Agents: measure the whole job

[Winning the Relay Race: Long-Running Agents in High-Value Customer Journeys](https://www.a16z.news/p/winning-the-relay-race-long-running) — **a16z News, 14 October 2026. Investor thesis.**

The essay argues that agents retaining context across a customer journey can address failures between individual tasks and make their contribution to an outcome more visible. It describes possible business models; it does not establish production reliability.

**Editorial implication:** evaluate one complete workflow, including interruptions and the final handoff. Count completed outcomes and required human interventions alongside task-level accuracy. A convincing individual step is insufficient evidence that the whole job succeeds.

## Distribution: investigate a specific channel

[Don't outsource growth to the algorithm](https://speedrun.substack.com/p/dont-outsource-growth-to-the-algorithm) — **Speedrun, 9 September 2026. Investor opinion; an older, evergreen selection.**

The essay encourages founders to understand emerging distribution channels and seek people who care about the problem, rather than relying on a stream of generic content. It discusses agents as another potential discovery channel.

**Editorial implication:** test whether a person or agent can find accurate product information and a clear next step. Measure discovery through to a useful action. This reading is selected for that question, not as an endorsement of every tactic in the essay.
